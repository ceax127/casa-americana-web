Our philosophy

Quiet luxury. Built to last.

We take the best of American homebuilding (open layouts, generous porches, the kitchen at the heart of the home) and adapt it to the Cajamarca valley: its altitude, its cool nights and its sunny days.

  • 100% steel structure: lighter, faster to build and safer in an earthquake.

  • Fixed price and a contractual schedule. No surprises.

Steel structure

Steel that protects what matters most.

Cajamarca sits in an active seismic zone. Every home and building we make stands on a 100% steel frame, the system of choice in earthquake country from California to Japan.

100% steel frame

Galvanized steel profiles with certified structural calculations.

Earthquake resistant

Steel is ductile: it flexes and absorbs a quake's energy instead of cracking.

Warm at altitude

Continuous insulation and low-E double glazing for cold mountain nights.

10-year warranty

Structural coverage by contract, plus first-year maintenance.

How to buy

Simple, clear terms. Full payment.

We sell with full payment only. If you need a loan, you arrange it directly with your bank.

  1. 1

    Reserve

    Hold your home or residence with a S/ 5,000 reservation. It is deducted from the price.

  2. 2

    Pay in full

    Complete the full payment within 30 days of your reservation, in cash or through your own bank.

  3. 3

    Sign and move in

    We sign the purchase contract and hand over your keys under the agreed schedule.

If full payment is not completed within 30 days of the reservation, the S/ 5,000 reservation is forfeited.
